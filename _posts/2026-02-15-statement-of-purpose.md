## 1) It's not a "motivational letter" 🚨

In Germany, this is often called a "Motivationsschreiben". It's not.

Don't make the mistake of arguing why your subject is the most fascinating discipline in the world or how you want to solve climate change and inequality with it. What they are looking for is academic excellence. It's much closer to an "Evidence of Academic Fit".

## 2) Main body structure 🩻

What specific content have you studied? Why did it fascinate you? How will you build on this in the Master's? When I say "what", I don't mean listing course names superficially. Talk about methods. Use technical language. The reader will understand it, trust me.

## 3) It's all about academics 🤓

They want specialists. Academic weapons. Irrelevant extracurriculars should usually be left out. Otherwise, it might look like you're not spending all day in the library (oh no). Social engagement is also not really relevant, unlike in scholarship applications.

Rule of thumb for music and sports: anything at national level is impressive and worth mentioning. You can then also pair it with the relevant Oxford society.

However, academically related extracurriculars are great! You can connect them with a strong passion for academics.

## 4) Don't make yourself smaller than you are ☀️

Many people don't want to sound arrogant, so they downplay their achievements. In real life, that makes you likeable. An application is not the place for that.

## 5) Put achievements into context 🤔

The reader needs to understand why something is highly compelling. Mention rankings if they work in your favour.

Rule of thumb: valedictorian to top 5% → mention it. Top 10% starts to become questionable, because you don't compete with the other 90%. You compete with the top 2%.

Also, the readers may not speak your language. They may not know what a "Bundesjugendorchester" is. But they will understand "the highly selective German National Youth Orchestra".

## 6) The Oxbridge punchline 🤯

Many applicants open with quotes, and that's fine. I now strongly prefer starting directly with my most relevant recent project. For example, my statement began with: "In my Bachelor's thesis, I [...]".

Absolutely avoid generic opening phrases you were taught in school.

## 7) A lot of time lies between the application and your Master's 🤐

Nothing forbids you from mentioning what you plan to do, even if you haven't done it yet.

## 8) If your future feels unclear, lean towards a PhD 👨🏻‍🎓

Academia needs next generations. My impression is that this is actively encouraged.

## Bonus: your academic CV

For Master's applications, a CV differs from a perfect business CV in two ways:

**9) You are allowed to fill two pages**, as long as they are filled with substance. 2️⃣

**10) You can list relevant coursework.** 🧮
